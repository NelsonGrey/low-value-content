# Code of Conduct

Low Value Content is satire about online life. The jokes target platforms, trends, and ourselves, never people for who they are. We hold our working space to a higher standard than our slogans.

## Our expectations

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Give and accept feedback on work, not on people.
- Assume good intent; ask before assuming.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality, including unreleased designs and business documents.
- Keep satire aimed at behaviors and systems, not at individuals or protected groups.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sharing others' private information, or deliberately undermining others' work.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository owner, [@mnelson3](https://github.com/mnelson3). The owner will review reports promptly and may remove access or contributions that violate this code.
