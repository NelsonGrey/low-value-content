# The Constitution of Engagement

Status: working canon for the Low Value Content fictional world. This document governs the website, lineage, calendar, regalia, titles, Houses, product provenance, and future QR experiences. It does not replace the commercial brand or legal seller identity.

## The central premise

The Royal House of Engagement governs the Attention Economy by converting attention into impressions, impressions into influence, and influence into proof of its own importance.

Low Value Content remains the consumer brand, merchant, portal, purveyor, and maker's mark. The Royal House of Engagement is the fictional institution encountered beyond that threshold.

## The state in ten principles

1. Attention is the natural resource.
2. Engagement is political legitimacy.
3. Impressions are the currency.
4. Influence is the aristocracy.
5. Content is the principal industry.
6. The Algorithm is divine providence.
7. Metrics are presumed accurate until they become inconvenient.
8. Brain rot is officially classified as Sustained Cognitive Engagement.
9. No subject may admit that nobody is paying attention.
10. More content is the official remedy for too much content.

The primary motto remains *Multa edita, pauca dicta* — Much Published, Little Said. Latin supplies ceremonial texture; primary names, navigation, jokes, and explanations remain in English.

## Constitutional hierarchy

1. **The Crown** embodies the dominant technological authority of the current reign.
2. **The Royal House of Engagement** claims permanent ownership of legitimacy in the Attention Economy.
3. **The Six Great Houses** compete to produce, distribute, measure, and certify importance.
4. **Ministries and Institutes** convert each House's contradiction into respectable policy, research, and administration.
5. **The Peerage** grants territorial titles for dubious distinction.
6. **The Orders** award honors across House boundaries.
7. **The Subjects** follow, create, refresh, and occasionally attempt to log off.

The Great Houses are constitutionally equal. Court precedence changes with each reign. Under Prompt I, current precedence is Prompt, Scroll, Influence, Signal, Spam, then Buffer. The precedence of Buffer remains pending.

## The Six Great Houses

### House of Prompt

- Portfolio: AI generation, synthetic expertise, and automated creativity.
- Contradiction: The less one understands, the more confidently one produces.
- Institution: Office of Synthetic Understanding.
- Motto: *Pete sine intellegendo* — Ask without understanding.
- Present function: Produces the content.

### House of Scroll

- Portfolio: Attention, compulsion, and infinite feeds.
- Contradiction: Nothing is important, but everything must be seen.
- Institution: Royal Institute of Digital Inquiry.
- Motto: *Semper deorsum* — Forever downward.
- Present function: Keeps it moving.

### House of Influence

- Portfolio: Status, visibility, personal brands, and public importance.
- Contradiction: Everyone is an influencer, leaving no one available to be influenced.
- Institution: Chancery of Public Importance.
- Motto: *Notus quia notus* — Known for being known.
- Present function: Declares it important.

### House of Signal

- Portfolio: Analytics, dashboards, attribution, and evidence.
- Contradiction: Every decision is data-driven; the data is adjusted to support the decision.
- Institution: Ministry of Measurable Outcomes.
- Working motto: *Quod mensuratur verum est* — What is measured is true.
- Present function: Proves it worked.

### House of Spam

- Portfolio: Scale, distribution, repetition, and unsolicited reach.
- Contradiction: If nobody responded, the audience was insufficiently large.
- Institution: Ministry of Universal Correspondence.
- Motto: *Ad omnes aeque* — To all subjects equally.
- Present function: Sends it to everyone else.

### House of Buffer

- Portfolio: Delay, loading, postponement, and technical excuses.
- Contradiction: Everything is imminent; nothing is presently available.
- Institution: Commission for Imminent Availability.
- Motto: *Tandem, fortasse* — Eventually, perhaps.
- Present function: Precedence pending.

All Latin remains working creative copy until reviewed for final production.

## Peerage rules

- **Duke or Duchess:** platform-scale compulsions and major cultural pathologies.
- **Marquess or Marchioness:** unstable boundaries between online and offline, truth and opinion, or human and synthetic.
- **Count or Countess:** metrics, followers, views, impressions, and anything misleadingly counted.
- **Viscount or Viscountess:** secondary statistics and derivative status.
- **Baron or Baroness:** everyday digital indignities and minor technical failures.

Titles describe the distinction. Houses describe the mechanism behind it. Institutions provide respectable justification. Orders recognize achievement across House boundaries.

Therefore the Duke of Doomscrolling belongs to the House of Scroll. His expanded ceremonial style may read:

> His Grace, the Duke of Doomscrolling
>
> First Peer of the House of Scroll
>
> Fellow of the Royal Institute of Digital Inquiry
>
> Companion of the Order of the Blue Check

This full style belongs in online provenance, packaging, or a certificate. Apparel uses only the portion that improves the garment.

## Orders and institutions

- **Order of the Blue Check:** a cross-House distinction for conspicuous personal visibility.
- **Order of Jacquard:** recognition for programmable instructions and ancestral automation.
- **The Universal Peerage:** the unresolved constitutional crisis created when every subject became an influencer and no uninfluenced population remained. The matter remains with committee.

## Rules for products

1. Every launch garment must work as a front-only design.
2. The original observation remains more important than the lore.
3. A product may have one primary Great House, one peerage title or institutional appointment, and one Order at most.
4. Public Notices require no House, title, or mythology.
5. A House is assigned by the mechanism being satirized, not merely by a related word.
6. A title names the behavior or distinction; it does not replace the joke.
7. An institution makes the absurdity respectable.
8. An Order rewards visibility, status, or ceremonial achievement across Houses.
9. Extended styles, history, and QR provenance belong online or in packaging unless they materially improve the shirt.
10. Concept mockups never override the canonical front-art master.

## Naming and presentation rule

Use English for the House, hierarchy, navigation, product names, institutions, titles, and explanations. Use Latin selectively on mottos, seals, warrants, medals, and subordinate ceremonial inscriptions, with an English translation available nearby. The joke must never depend on translation.
