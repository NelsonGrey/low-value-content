# The Customs of Engagement

Status: working canon for the Low Value Content fictional world. This document governs the website, lineage, calendar, regalia, titles, Houses, product provenance, and future QR experiences. It does not replace the commercial brand or legal seller identity.

*Formerly titled "The Constitution of Engagement". Renamed on November 23, 2026. The realm has no government and no constitution. Its rules are customs: unwritten, set informally by the few who can enforce them, and amended without notice. Nothing in this document describes a neutral, independent, or legitimate state, and the House never admits that it does not.*

## The central premise

The Crown holds dominion over the realm's attention, converting attention into impressions, impressions into influence, and influence into proof of its own importance. It reigns by the favour of whichever House can hold the room.

Low Value Content remains the consumer brand, merchant, portal, purveyor, and maker's mark. The Royal House of Engagement is the fictional institution encountered beyond that threshold.

## The realm in ten customs

1. Attention is the natural resource.
2. Engagement is legitimacy.
3. Impressions are the currency.
4. Influence is the aristocracy.
5. Content is the principal industry.
6. The Algorithm is divine providence.
7. Metrics are presumed accurate until they become inconvenient.
8. Brain rot is officially classified as Sustained Cognitive Engagement.
9. No subject may admit that nobody is paying attention.
10. More content is the official remedy for too much content.

These customs are unwritten. They bind the subjects, and they are amended, without notice, by whoever is able to enforce the amendment.

The primary motto remains *Multa edita, pauca dicta* — Much Published, Little Said. Latin supplies ceremonial texture; primary names, navigation, declarations, and explanations remain in English.

## The order of precedence

1. **The Crown** embodies the dominant technological authority of the current reign.
2. **The Royal House of Engagement** claims permanent ownership of legitimacy in the realm of attention.
3. **The Six Great Houses** compete to produce, distribute, measure, and certify importance. They are the kingmakers: none holds the Crown, but no sovereign reigns without the favour of at least one of them.
4. **Offices and Institutes** convert each House's contradiction into respectable decree, research, and custom.
5. **The Peerage** grants territorial titles for dubious distinction.
6. **The Orders** award honors across House boundaries.
7. **The Subjects** follow, create, refresh, and occasionally attempt to log off.

The Great Houses are equal by custom. Court precedence changes with each reign. Under Prompt the Confident, current precedence is Prompt, Scroll, Influence, Signal, Spam, then Buffer. The precedence of Buffer remains pending.

## The Six Great Houses

### House of Prompt

- Province: AI generation, synthetic expertise, and automated creativity.
- Contradiction: The less one understands, the more confidently one produces.
- Institution: Office of Synthetic Understanding.
- Motto: *Pete sine intellegendo* — Ask without understanding.
- Present function: Produces the content.

### House of Scroll

- Province: Attention, compulsion, and infinite feeds.
- Contradiction: Nothing is important, but everything must be seen.
- Institution: Royal Institute of Digital Inquiry.
- Motto: *Semper deorsum* — Forever downward.
- Present function: Keeps it moving.

### House of Influence

- Province: Status, visibility, personal brands, and public importance.
- Contradiction: Everyone is an influencer, leaving no one available to be influenced.
- Institution: Chancery of Public Importance.
- Motto: *Notus quia notus* — Known for being known.
- Present function: Declares it important.

### House of Signal

- Province: Analytics, dashboards, attribution, and evidence.
- Contradiction: Every decision is data-driven; the data is adjusted to support the decision.
- Institution: Exchequer of Measurable Outcomes.
- Working motto: *Quod mensuratur verum est* — What is measured is true.
- Present function: Proves it worked.

### House of Spam

- Province: Scale, distribution, repetition, and unsolicited reach.
- Contradiction: If nobody responded, the audience was insufficiently large.
- Institution: Heralds of Universal Correspondence.
- Motto: *Ad omnes aeque* — To all subjects equally.
- Present function: Sends it to everyone else.

### House of Buffer

- Province: Delay, loading, postponement, and technical excuses.
- Contradiction: Everything is imminent; nothing is presently available.
- Institution: Commission for Imminent Availability.
- Motto: *Tandem, fortasse* — Eventually, perhaps.
- Present function: Precedence pending.

All Latin remains working creative copy until reviewed for final production.

*Institutions are respectable fronts. The Houses hold the power; the Offices, Exchequer, Heralds, Chancery, and Institutes give it a presentable name. Offices exist to provide the official interpretation of what a House has already done.*

## Peerage rules

- **Duke or Duchess:** platform-scale compulsions and major cultural pathologies.
- **Marquess or Marchioness:** unstable boundaries between online and offline, truth and opinion, or human and synthetic.
- **Count or Countess:** metrics, followers, views, impressions, and anything misleadingly counted.
- **Viscount or Viscountess:** secondary statistics and derivative status.
- **Baron or Baroness:** everyday digital indignities and minor technical failures.

Titles describe the distinction. Houses describe the mechanism behind it. Institutions provide respectable justification. Orders recognize achievement across House boundaries.

Therefore the Duke of Doomscrolling belongs to the House of Scroll. His expanded ceremonial style may read:

> His Grace, the Duke of Doomscrolling
>
> First Peer of the House of Scroll
>
> Fellow of the Royal Institute of Digital Inquiry
>
> Companion of the Order of the Blue Check

This full style belongs in online provenance, packaging, or a certificate. Apparel uses only the portion that improves the garment.

## Orders and institutions

- **Order of the Blue Check:** a cross-House distinction for conspicuous personal visibility.
- **Order of Jacquard:** recognition for programmable instructions and ancestral automation.
- **The Universal Peerage:** the unresolved crisis of the realm created when every subject became an influencer and no uninfluenced population remained. The matter remains before the Privy Council.

## Rules for products

1. Every launch garment must work as a front-only design.
2. The original observation remains more important than the lore.
3. A product may have one primary Great House, one peerage title or institutional appointment, and one Order at most.
4. Public Notices require no House, title, or mythology.
5. A House is assigned by the mechanism being satirized, not merely by a related word.
6. A title names the behavior or distinction; it does not replace the primary declaration.
7. An institution establishes the official interpretation.
8. An Order rewards visibility, status, or ceremonial achievement across Houses.
9. Extended styles, history, and QR provenance belong online or in packaging unless they materially improve the shirt.
10. Concept mockups never override the canonical front-art master.

## Naming and presentation rule

Use English for the House, hierarchy, navigation, product names, institutions, titles, and explanations. Use Latin selectively on mottos, seals, warrants, medals, and subordinate ceremonial inscriptions, with an English translation available nearby. Meaning must never depend on translation.

## Voice and editorial discipline

The House speaks as a sovereign court and accepts its own authority without qualification.

1. Never describe the work as a joke, satire, parody, absurdity, or fiction in public-facing copy.
2. Never explain why a line is amusing or signal that the institution recognizes its own excesses.
3. State improbable doctrine with administrative precision and complete seriousness.
4. Prefer decrees, findings, classifications, records, appointments, and official interpretations.
5. Permit contradiction to remain unresolved. The House records the contradiction; it does not comment upon it.
6. Keep commercial facts direct. Price, availability, sizing, payment, fulfillment, returns, privacy, and customer rights remain outside ceremonial ambiguity.
7. **Never present the realm's rules as neutral, independent, democratic, or the work of a government.** The customs are unwritten and set by those able to enforce them. The House records them without admitting who sets them.
8. **Speak as a court, not as a state.** Avoid the vocabulary of a civil state (government, constitution, ministry, committee, policy, economy, administration, elected). Prefer the vocabulary of a court: Crown, Court, favour, precedence, appointment, decree, writ, privy council, exchequer, chancery, heralds, province, charge. Words from the modern internet (algorithm, scroll, prompt, metrics, dashboard) keep their names; they are the subject of the satire.
