## Shared blocks (every product page)

**Garment.** Printed to order on a Comfort Colors 1717 garment-dyed tee: 6.1 oz pre-shrunk ring-spun cotton, relaxed fit, double-needle stitching, no side seams, made in Honduras. Colors are Ivory, True Navy and Brick, and each product page names the color it is sold in. [Size chart below, from the sample.] The artwork is printed on the front only.

**Care.** Machine wash cold (no warmer than 30 °C / 90 °F), inside out, with like colors. Do not bleach. Tumble dry low or hang to dry. If ironing, use low heat inside out and never directly on the print. Do not dry clean. (From Printify's care instructions for this blank; confirm against the sewn-in label on the sample.)

**Made to order.** Each shirt is printed after you order it, which is why none sit in a warehouse. Production takes [2 to 5, unverified] business days before it ships.

- **How long it takes.** [2 to 5] business days to produce, then [3 to 7] business days in transit within the US. A tracking link is emailed when it ships.
- **Returns.** Every shirt is made to order, so we cannot accept returns for fit or change of mind. **Proposed:** if an item arrives damaged, misprinted or is not the item ordered, we replace it or refund it in full when you contact us within 30 days of delivery with a photograph.

Decisions for you: the return window (30 days proposed), whether to offer free exchanges for the wrong size, and whether to ship beyond the US at launch.
