## Shipping and returns (draft policy, needs your decisions)

These are proposals, not policy. Printify and the provider set the real production and transit times, so every number below needs confirming against the chosen provider.

- **Where we ship.** The United States only, at launch. Shipping is a flat $5.99 (the figure already configured at checkout).
- **How long it takes.** [2 to 5] business days to produce, then [3 to 7] business days in transit within the US. A tracking link is emailed when it ships.
- **Returns.** Every shirt is made to order, so we cannot accept returns for fit or change of mind. **Proposed:** if an item arrives damaged, misprinted or is not the item ordered, we replace it or refund it in full when you contact us within 30 days of delivery with a photograph.
- **Sizing.** A size chart sits on each product page. Because the garment runs [relaxed], we suggest ordering your usual size.
- **Cancellations.** An order can be cancelled until it enters production, usually within [a few hours] of ordering.

Decisions for you: the return window (30 days proposed), whether to offer free exchanges for the wrong size, and whether to ship beyond the US at launch.
