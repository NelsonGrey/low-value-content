# Realm Artwork Mockup Record

These four storefront mockups complete the visual ten-shirt founding wardrobe. They are concept images for design evaluation, not print-ready production artwork.

The subsequent front/back merger pilot and its governing rules are documented in [`realm-garment-system-v2.md`](realm-garment-system-v2.md).

## Shared art direction

- Portrait 4:5 individual product photography
- Heavyweight crew-neck shirt on a dark wooden tailor's mannequin
- Warm, restrained old-world studio with neutral stone and plaster
- Soft museum lighting and an intentionally over-serious luxury presentation
- Centered, screen-print-conscious heraldic artwork
- No person, watermark, visible brand label, or unrelated copy

## No. 07 — Much Published, Little Said

## No. 09 — The End of the World Has Been Postponed

Create a deep oxblood Millennium Interregnum issue: gold cartouche, split digital clock reading `99 | 00`, hourglass, crossed computer cables, crown, and the exact texts `THE END OF THE WORLD HAS BEEN POSTPONED` and `FINIS MUNDI DILATUS`.

## No. 10 — Patron Saint of Debugging

Create a warm-ivory Feast of Saint Moth issue: natural-history moth, circuit-trace halo, laurel branches, vintage relay, restrained navy accents, and the exact texts `PATRON SAINT OF DEBUGGING` and `PRIMUS CASUS VERUS`.
