Status: October 31, 2026. Built from the apparel manifest, the sayings review and the live site. Companion to [sayings-review.md](sayings-review.md).

The Wave 2 shortlist in the sayings review fixes most of this: it adds four Spam lines, three Buffer lines, a Scroll inscription, and five commemorations on LO, the Content Creator, Mandatory Emotional Context, the Infinite Scroll and Human Obsolescence.

## Quantity

- **Launch assortment:** 10 founding issues plus a Wave 2 of 10 gives 20 designs. That is enough to look like a House rather than a drop, and small enough to sample properly.
- **Calendar engine:** each of the 12 observances is a dated commemoration. One a month after launch gives a year of content without inventing new concepts, and every release has a reason to exist on its date.
- **Order of release:** founding ten first, then Wave 2 in two groups of five (one per House where possible), then commemorations on their dates.

## Quality gaps on the site

- Product pages have one line of copy. They still lack fit, fabric and care, a size chart, and a short note on how the shirt is made. These depend on the garment blank, which is not confirmed.
- No real legal pages (Terms, Privacy, Cookies are pre-launch placeholders) and no returns or shipping copy.
- Social links go to a "plans" page; the accounts do not exist.
- Only some products have a studio mannequin mockup. The rest show flat art.
- No email sign-up for launch, no Open Graph tags, sitemap or robots file.

## What is blocked on a decision

- Garment blank and provider (Printify). Fit, fabric, size chart, colour names and print-area template all depend on it.
- Print-resolution art for Nos. 07-10: needs either an artist/AI upscale pass or a vector redraw. It cannot be produced from the current files without quality loss.
- Which ten Wave 2 sayings to take forward.
- Trademark searches on the brand name, the House name, the tagline and the final ten sayings.
