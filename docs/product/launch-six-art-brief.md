Status: draft for Mark's review, November 10, 2026. Written to be handed to an outside AI image/design tool that has **not** seen this repository. Everything it needs is in this file; attach the reference files named under each shirt. Nothing here is trademark-cleared.

## 1. The job

Turn six approved sayings into print-ready front artwork for t-shirts, each placed correctly inside the fictional world ("the Realm") the brand uses. The brand is **Low Value Content**, a satirical editorial apparel label. Its fictional institution is **The Royal House of Engagement**, a mock monarchy that governs the attention economy. The tone is **over-serious**: the joke is in the words and the objects, and the art never winks. No cartoons, no memes, no smiley faces.

## 1a. We want new designs, not corrected copies

**Read this before anything else.** An earlier pass on this brief audited the existing prototypes and reproduced them with small corrections (recolored a red, fixed a margin, changed one word, removed a mark). That is **not** what we are asking for. The existing files are the product of a first draft; the sayings are approved, but the **artwork is open for a genuine redesign**.

What this means:

- **Fixed:** the exact wording, the garment color and two inks for each shirt, the Realm rules in section 2, the print constraints in section 3, and the front-only rule.
- **Open, and where we want your thinking:** the layout, the typographic treatment, the hero visual idea, the hierarchy, the proportions, the details and the graphic device that makes each joke land. Come up with a fresh concept for each shirt.
- **The "Composition" lines in section 4 describe the old design.** Treat them as a record of what exists and as a starting point to move away from, not as a specification. If your concept is stronger, use it. Where a line says "Keep off", that is a rule; where it says "Composition", it is a suggestion.
- **The "Existing files" lines show what we already have.** Use them to understand the brand's tone and the frame and imprint convention. Do not trace, restyle or lightly rearrange them. A design whose first impression is "the old file with a tweak" will be rejected.
- **Every shirt should pass the two-second test as its own idea.** One strong hero object or typographic move, readable at about 200 px wide, that a stranger would recognise as that specific joke.
- **Keep the set coherent:** six distinct ideas that clearly belong together through the palette, type discipline and the shared House frame and imprint, not six variations of one template.
- **Propose before polishing.** For each shirt, show two or three distinct concepts as rough comps first (we will pick), then finish the chosen one to print spec. Do not spend effort on print-resolution finish for a concept we have not chosen.

## 2. The Realm in one page

**Three registers.** Every design belongs to exactly one:

- **Public Notice**: a contemporary editorial or interface-style typographic design. One or two inks. Looks like a system message, form, or status screen. **No heraldry, no crowns, no coats of arms, no Latin.** It may carry a small framed imprint line only.
- **Court & Order**: authority, appointment, rank, medals, institutes. Formal type, a seal or one restrained heraldic device. Latin only if subordinate.
- **Royal Commemoration**: dated technology history in full heraldry. (None of the six.)

**Six Great Houses**, each a branch of government with one charge (symbol): Prompt (command chevron and waiting cursor), **Scroll** (descending feed and arrow), **Influence** (radiant faceted star), **Signal** (measured bars and ascending trace), Spam (repeated envelopes), Buffer (incomplete loading ring). Badges share one frame: crown, double rule, botanical supporters, navy shield, ivory face, gold line, oxblood charge.

**Hard rules**
1. The **peacock, parrot, notification shield and Wi-Fi crown belong to the Crown only**. None of the six shirts uses them. Never put them on a Public Notice, and do not substitute a similar bird, crest or shield.
2. Public Notices stay typographic. Four of the six (01, 04, 05, 06) are Public Notices.
3. **Front print only.** No sleeve, back, neck or QR code on the garment. The design must be complete on its own.
4. **One hero idea, centered, readable at 200 px wide** (the "two-second test"). If it needs a caption to be funny, it is not done.
5. **No real people, companies, logos, platform interface trade dress or protected slogans.** Abstract the interface; do not copy any real app.
6. English is the joke. Latin and dates are quiet secondary texture. **Do not invent Latin.** None of these six needs any.
7. Keep text short and **exact**. Do not add, rephrase, or "improve" any wording.

**Palette** (garments are only Ivory, Oxblood, Navy, Black; no editorial black, lime or notification red in the ink set):

| Name | Hex |
|---|---|
| Archive navy | #142B40 |
| Oxblood | #852E34 |
| Antique gold | #BD9143 (use #C99B43 on dark garments) |
| Ivory | #E7D9BA (art) / #F4EEDF (garment) |

**Type.** Headlines keep their own register (grotesque, typewriter, report, appointment serif). Small text is a classical serif (Georgia-like) in wide-tracked capitals. The shared frame for the Public Notices and Court & Orders is a double rule with diamond corners and the imprint line `THE ROYAL HOUSE OF ENGAGEMENT · NO. NN`, in gold, small. See the reference SVGs for exact construction.

## 3. Print constraints (these decide what is acceptable)

- Printing is **DTG (direct to garment)** on a Comfort Colors 1717 tee. One chest print, about **12 x 15 in**. The Printify front print area is **3703 x 4200 px** on the current provider (3425 to 3709 wide on others), so 4500 x 5400 files are fitted to the height; keep important content away from the outer 3%.
- **Deliverable per shirt:** a **transparent-background** file, **4500 x 5400 px** (300 dpi at 15 in tall), sRGB. All six are two-ink designs, so a **vector SVG master** is required and the PNG is rendered from it.
- Only the two named inks plus transparency. **Garment-colour knockouts are not an ink**; a "knockout" word is simply transparent.
- Minimum line weight about 0.03 in (about 10 px at 4500 wide); minimum type about 0.2 in tall. Nothing finer than that will survive the print.
- No drop shadows, glows, gradients on two-ink designs, textures baked into the art, or mockup backgrounds. Flat, crisp, screen-print-like edges.
- Convert live type to outlines in the print file; keep an editable copy.
- Dark garments get a white underbase from the printer, so keep colour areas solid, not feathered.

### No. 04: Content creator. (audit lines)
- **Form / Realm:** Public Notice, no lore, no House. A deadpan account audit of a creator's career. Website-only lore: Content the Wanting (a sovereign), which stays off the shirt.
- **Garment / inks:** **Ivory**; navy + oxblood. The ivory garment reads as the paper of an audit, and the oxblood status flags read as red marks on it.
- **Text (exact):** `CONTENT CREATOR.` / `Uniqueness questionable.` / `Monetization inconsistent.` / `Visibility unreported.` (no footer; the old "MEANING PENDING" line is dropped). Alternates for the third line, if wanted: `Attention oversubscribed.` or `Market saturated.`

A thin rule closes the ledger. The point of the design is the three-beat parallel structure; do not add a chart (that is No. 06's device) and keep it visibly different from No. 06's report layout.
- **The idea behind the three lines:** the first implies a crowded market (if it were unique it would not be questionable), the second is the income, and the third is the platform never reporting whether anyone saw it. Keep the three flags visibly different (for example hollow, half-filled, and an empty dotted marker for "unreported"). No real platform's icons or analytics screens.
- **Keep off:** crowns, heraldry, Latin, a real platform's metrics screen, a monospace typewriter look copied from the old draft.
- **Existing files (context only, do not reproduce):** `design/prototypes/04-content-creator.svg` shows the **old** wording and layout; use it only to see the frame and imprint (`THE ROYAL HOUSE OF ENGAGEMENT · NO. 04`).

## 5. Variety check across the six

Garments are assigned two each of ivory, oxblood and navy, chosen for the joke rather than for the old files:

- **Ivory (01, 04):** the paper of a form and an audit. 01 is a request form; 04 is a ledger whose oxblood flags read as red marks. Navy + oxblood inks.
- **Oxblood (03, 05):** 03 is the institute seal and the Realm's own colour; 05 turns gold into the price tag, with MONETIZED knocked out of a gold block. Ivory + antique gold inks.
- **Navy (02, 06):** 02 is the formal court appointment, where gold on navy is the classic seal; 06 is 2 AM, so night. Ivory + antique gold inks.

The set should read as one wardrobe but not six copies of one layout. Keep 04 and 06 visibly different: 04 is a centered ledger of three verdicts, 06 is a quiet left-aligned message log. Four of the six are on dark garments, so the printer will lay a white underbase; keep colour areas solid and avoid very fine detail in light-on-dark type.
