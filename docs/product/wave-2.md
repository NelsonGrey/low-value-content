## How each is built

All Wave 2 designs are Public Notice or Court & Order forms, so they use the **vector two-ink pipeline**: an SVG in `design/prototypes/`, the shared House frame and imprint, the House mark, rendered with `scripts/render-front-art.py`. This is the cheapest and most reliable pipeline: it prints sharply at any size and needs no upscaling. Full-colour plates (the heraldic art of Nos. 07-10) are reserved for Royal Commemorations and the occasional Court & Order of great standing.
