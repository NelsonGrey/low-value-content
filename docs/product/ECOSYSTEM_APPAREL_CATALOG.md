# Ecosystem Apparel Catalog

Status: working commercial-creative source of truth. Nothing in this document is trademark-cleared, marketplace-cleared, provider-approved, sampled, or authorized for sale unless a later production record says so.

## What this catalog resolves

The project contains 100 original concepts. Six were promoted into the Founding Ten, and four Realm-native products were added. The complete ecosystem therefore contains **104 unique product concepts**: ten Founding products plus ninety-four unpromoted expansion concepts.

This catalog connects every concept to a printable issue form, the constitutional mechanism it belongs to, a website destination, and the imagery direction. Machine-readable data lives in [`ecosystem-apparel-catalog.json`](ecosystem-apparel-catalog.json).

## The four printable issue forms

| Code | Issue form | Garment rule | Website role |
|---|---|---|---|
| PN | Public Notice | Front-only; typography or one familiar interface object; 1–2 inks; no sovereign heraldry; three short lines maximum. | Direct observation; Regalia and future subject collections |
| HW | House Warrant | Front-only; one large recognizable object plus one subordinate House badge; 2–4 inks; no sovereign supporters. | Realm and the relevant Great House |
| CO | Court & Order | Front-only; centered seal, medal, certificate, or appointment device; 2–5 inks; English remains primary and Latin is subordinate. | Realm, peerage, institutions, and Orders |
| RC | Royal Commemoration | Front-only; dated relic, specimen, or historical device; limited-color or approved full-color art; English title plus optional short Latin line. | Calendar, Lineage, and commemorative Regalia |

Atmospheric photography follows [`IMAGERY_DIRECTION.md`](../brand/IMAGERY_DIRECTION.md). Print art is built first and remains separate from the photographic mockup. Public Notices never receive sovereign heraldry.

## Production sequence

1. Lock exact wording and issue form.
2. Create or refine isolated front artwork; no garment or room.
3. Test at 200 px for recognition and at provider dimensions for legibility.
4. Convert approved artwork to an editable, separation-ready production master.
5. Place that exact master on the provider template.
6. Generate concept photography using the locked artwork as the reference.
7. Order a physical sample; inspect placement, underbase, fine lines, color, fit, and wash behavior.
8. Only then change commerce status or describe the item as available.

## Catalog-wide acceptance rules

- The observation must read before the lore.
- One front print area at launch.
- One hero object, recognizable in two seconds, when imagery is used.
- Three short lines of primary text at most; longer provenance belongs online or in packaging.
- Public Notices use no sovereign arms, supporters, or decorative crown.
- House Warrant badges remain subordinate to the declaration.
- Court and Commemoration designs may use ornate imagery only when the object is immediately recognizable.
- English carries meaning; Latin is optional texture with a nearby translation online.
- Generated artwork is never treated as print-ready solely because it looks finished.
- Every item retains open rights, provider, sample, and commerce gates until those reviews are documented.
