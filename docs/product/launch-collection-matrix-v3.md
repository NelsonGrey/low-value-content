# Launch Collection Matrix V3

## Governing principle

The Realm is an optional source of authority, not a mandatory classification system. A design enters its jurisdiction only when formal provenance strengthens the observation.

All launch garments begin as **front-only prints**. House seals, QR destinations, titles, lineage, and calendar material may appear on the product page, hangtag, packaging insert, or later premium edition without increasing the initial garment's print-area cost.

## Collection architecture

| Collection | Purpose | Visual language | Realm intensity |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Public Notices** | Immediate social-media observations | Contemporary editorial typography, diagrams, status language, restrained monochrome | None to light |
| **Court & Orders** | Status, appointment, rank, and institutional authority | Formal typography, seals, medals, circular institutes, selective heraldry | Moderate to full |
| **Royal Commemorations** | Technology history transformed into ceremonial observance | Heraldry, Latin, dates, relics, natural-history and archival devices | Full |

## Launch constraints

- One front print area per garment.
- One approved garment blank and Printify provider before expanding variants.
- Generated mockups remain concept references, not upload-ready artwork.
- Every selected design requires editable production artwork, provider-template placement, sample order, print inspection, fit review, and wash testing.
- Do not print a QR code on the launch garment until physical scan testing establishes a reliable minimum size, contrast, placement, curvature tolerance, and post-wash performance.
- Realm information may be extensive online while remaining minimal or absent on the shirt.
